About

I'm a Berlin-based composer, artist and audio engineer working across contemporary dance, electronic music and live performance.

Writing for contemporary dance is where I want to go furthest. Recent work includes ICH, EURYDIKE at Staatsoperette Dresden and Next to Me in Regensburg, both with choreographer Gabriel Pitoni.

With Skew Krew, I play keys and samples live — somewhere between hypnotic techno, UK break and experimental club music. Our EP SADKOWO landed on 6 March 2026, with shows at Arkaoda, Renate, Paloma and Traumburg Festival.
